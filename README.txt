SEC 1 STUDY TRACKER - iPhone / website version

PUT IT ON THE INTERNET (free, about 5 minutes)
1. Unzip this file. You get a folder called "sec-1-web".
2. Go to https://app.netlify.com/drop in a browser and drag the whole "sec-1-web" folder onto the page.
   (Netlify may ask you to create a free account to keep the site. Do it, otherwise it can expire.)
3. Netlify gives you an address ending in .netlify.app. Open it to check it works.

ADD IT TO THE IPHONE HOME SCREEN
1. Open that address in SAFARI on the iPhone (it must be Safari).
2. Tap the Share button, then "Add to Home Screen", then Add.
3. Open it from the new icon on the Home Screen, then create your account THERE.
   (The Home Screen app and Safari keep separate data, so an account made in Safari will not appear in the app.)

UPDATING LATER
In Netlify open your site > Deploys > drag the new folder onto the page. Open the app twice to see the update.

Your account and progress are stored only on that device. Passwords cannot be recovered.
